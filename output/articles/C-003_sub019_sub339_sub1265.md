Location & Transportation
Location
The Pingtung Technology Industrial Park (PTIP) lies in the southwest of Pingtung(屏東) City, Pingtung County in southern Taiwan. It is located at the border between Pingtung County and Kaohsiung City and covers 123.04 hectares. County highway No. 189 divides the zone into two parts, east and west. The east side of the zone is located next to the Cingshuei River(清水溪); the west side faces the Gaoping River(高屏溪). The zone faces Wandan(萬丹) Town on the south, and Tai No. 1 provincial highway is 200 meters on the north.
Sufficient Water and Power Supply
A. Water Supply
A maximum daily supply of 50,000 CMD.
B. Power Supply
An available voltage of 161KV and the maximum power supply of 480,000KW with 22.8KV.
Single-stop & High Efficiency.
Public Infrastructure 
Well-planned Environment and Quality Service for Business Investors
Four Major Sections of the Pingtung Technology Industrial Park
A. Plants Section (72.10 ha): Providing land for the business types under the provision of Article 3 “Export Processing Zone Management Regulations”
B. Service Section (3.87 ha): Providing land for the use of MOEA Export Processing Administration in Pingtung and relevant competent agencies as well as a large conference room, product exhibit rooms, cafeteria and food court, convenience stores, basement parking lots, the operation of relevant business, and the police teams.
C. Public Infrastructure Section (45.78ha): Mainly for the use of substations, water supply plant, waste disposal plant, parks, transportations, retention runoff retarding facilities, telecommunications control room, and the leisure area.
D. Residential Section (1.29 ha): For the residence of the employees of the companies in the Technology Industrial Park, the Management Bureau of the Technology Industrial Park, or any business organization in the Technology Industrial Park.
Pingtung TIP-Transportation
A. By land：It takes 25 minutes to connect with national highway No.3 (Southern Second Freeway), approximately 10 kilometers north of the zone. The East-West Direction Fast Highway No. 88 is located about six kilometers away from the zone. Go west for 15 kilometers to national highway No. 1 (Sun Yat-sen National Freeway) and then take Tai 1st Line west to Kaohsiung and east to downtown Pingtung.
B.By sea：Drive southward and take East-West Direction Fast Highway No. 88 for 35 minutes to arrive at Kaohsiung Harbor.
C.By air：Drive southward for 21 kilometers, take the East-West Direction Fast Highway No.88, within 30 minutes to arrive at Kaohsiung International Airport. 
As the estimated figures indicate, it only takes 25 to 35 minutes to get to the above-mentioned destinations.
