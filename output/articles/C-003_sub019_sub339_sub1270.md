# Bureau of Industrial Parks, Ministry of Economic Affairs- Investment Requirements

- 原始網址：https://www.bip.gov.tw/english/page.aspx?pageid=20eba8186eabcf1d
- 網站：www.bip.gov.tw
- 關鍵字：待人工補充

(一)Minimum paid-in capital(in NTD)
1. $10 million for manufacturing businesses, if a plant is rented or purchased；
2. $20 million for manufacturing businesses, if land is rented for constructing a plant；
3. $80 million for warehousing businesses；
4. $50 million for transportation businesses；
5. $1 million for other businesses.
(二)Conforming to the Business Classifications Allowed to Be Established in the Technology Industrial Park.
